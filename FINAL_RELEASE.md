# AegisPay 6.0 — Final Release Notes

## Phase 5
Advanced Analytics, Risk Profiles, Security Center, fraud/risk distribution, bank and payment-method intelligence, customer/merchant profiling, security posture, alerts and audit activity.

## Phase 6
Reports with CSV export, System Health, runtime diagnostics, service matrix, Admin Center controls, governance presentation and responsive enterprise UI.

## Preserved Phase 4
New Payment, bank selection, payment methods, live payments, details, fraud scoring, incidents, investigation/re-investigation, approve/block, recovery, merchants, MongoDB and SSE.

## Demo recommendation
For the pitch, show one successful transaction, one suspicious/fraud transaction that becomes an incident, investigate/re-investigate it, recover an eligible held transaction, then finish with Security Center, Risk Profiles, Analytics, Reports and System Health.

## Important
Create `backend/.env` locally from `backend/.env.example` and use your own MongoDB credentials. Do not commit or share `.env`.
